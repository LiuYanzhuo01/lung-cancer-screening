Why Multimodal PET/CT Matters

CT alone excels at depicting structure, but early malignant changes can be subtle or ambiguous. PET adds metabolic context that may reveal abnormal activity before clear morphological signs appear. By learning from both modalities jointly, the system can potentially reduce false negatives in early screening while maintaining clinically acceptable specificity.

Potential Clinical Impact

For Screening Workflows

  • Assist radiologists by flagging high-risk nodules that warrant closer review.
  • Provide a quantitative risk score alongside conventional visual assessment.
  • Support triage in high-volume screening settings where reading time is limited.

For Early Detection Research

  • Demonstrate the value of fusing functional and anatomical imaging features.
  • Establish a reproducible pipeline for multimodal lung cancer AI studies.
  • Highlight the importance of sensitivity-focused evaluation in screening contexts.
Note: This website is a research project showcase. The system described is intended for research and educational purposes and has not been approved for clinical diagnostic use. Any clinical application would require rigorous validation, regulatory review, and prospective trials.
