Early Lung Cancer Screening System Based on Deep Learning and PET/CT Multimodal Features

A research-oriented framework for improving early detection through multimodal imaging and intelligent analysis.

Overview Method Results & Impact

Project Motivation

Lung cancer remains one of the leading causes of cancer-related mortality worldwide. Early-stage detection dramatically improves survival rates, yet traditional screening methods face challenges in sensitivity, specificity, and accessibility. This project explores a deep learning–based system that fuses PET metabolic information with CT anatomical detail to support earlier, more accurate screening decisions.

Deep Learning

Convolutional and multimodal neural networks learn complex imaging patterns associated with early malignant changes.

PET/CT Fusion

Combining functional PET signals with structural CT data provides complementary information for lesion characterization.

Early Detection Focus

The system targets subtle, early-stage nodules that are often missed or misclassified in single-modality workflows.

Core objective: To design and evaluate a multimodal deep learning pipeline that assists clinicians in identifying high-risk pulmonary nodules at the earliest possible stage, using routinely acquired PET/CT data.
